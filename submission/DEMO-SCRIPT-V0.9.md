## Technical explanation

The Quran contains over six thousand verses. Finding the right one from live recitation is challenging. Phrases repeat across different passages, and a listener can join halfway through a verse.

Meta’s hardware and AI work together here.

The glasses capture the recitation. Muse Voice turns that audio into a live Arabic transcript.

Our software searches the Quran reference and tracks the listener’s position, even inside a long verse.

Muse Spark helps resolve uncertain matches and pair Arabic portions with published English meaning.

Then we deliver readable portions to the small glasses display, updating automatically as the recitation continues.

Our software checks that the displayed wording comes directly from the published sources.

Spark’s pairing plans are cached for these demonstrations. Transcription and passage tracking run live.
