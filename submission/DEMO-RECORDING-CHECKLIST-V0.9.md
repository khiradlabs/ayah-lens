# Ayah Lens demo recording checklist 0.9

Faraz accepted these three shots on November 3. Masjid footage is cancelled. Capture three separate original recordings; each can fit within the current one-minute display-recording limit. Keep the original full files for editing.

## Setup once

Wear the glasses. Keep Ayah Lens open on the unlocked iPhone. Use Start Muse live Quran with your working TV/external reciter. Begin actual display recording after recognition is running. Keep Arabic and English readable; keep the phone version/build visible in a short setup shot if convenient. Do not change apps during the take. Capture the phone preview separately if available; the native glasses recording is the main proof.

## 1. TV following

Record 30–40 seconds for a final selection of about 20–25 seconds. Have the TV in front. English is on. Let two or more verse/portion transitions occur. Do not tap Next or manually change the passage. Preserve the recitation audio and visible automatic updates.

Narration cue before the uninterrupted proof: Watch the reciter on television. As the recitation continues, Ayah Lens automatically follows the passage and brings its meaning into view.

## 2. Tracking inside one long verse

Use Al-Muzzammil 73:20. Record 20–30 seconds with at least two automatic portion changes inside the same verse. Keep the 73:20 reference visible if the actual layout includes it. Do not press Next or scroll manually. Target a final 12–15-second extract.

Narration cue on a silent setup/hold: Watch the verse number. It stays the same. Ayah Lens is following our position inside this single long verse.

## 3. English off, then on

Record about 20 seconds. Start with English visible. Turn Show English meaning off once; allow Arabic to keep following for several seconds. Turn it on again and hold long enough to see English return. Only this shot includes deliberate control use. Target a final 8–10-second extract.

Narration cue on a silent setup/hold: For Hifz, or Quran memorization, you can turn off English and keep Arabic in view while rehearsing. Turn the meaning back on whenever you want to follow it.

## Handoff

Place all three original files in Downloads and tell the editor they are ready. Suggested names: ayah-tv-following, ayah-long-verse-73-20, ayah-english-toggle. Keep originals untrimmed. No other recording is required by this checklist.

## Editor acceptance check

Verify actual source, version where visible, duration, readable Arabic/English, genuine automatic transitions, and audible recitation. Inspect that toggle changes are actual control use, not missing alignment. Do not call a native camera/display composite optical through-lens footage. Do not manufacture a successful transition from a stalled take. Keep source speed at 1x. Let recitation play without competing English narration. The 40–50-second final demo allocation remains provisional until the complete narration is timed under 180 seconds.
