# Ayah Lens video script Version 1

The impact-led direction discussed with Faraz. Version 2 develops this direction against the judging rubric.

## Opening narration

About two billion people are Muslim.

For many of us, Arabic is not our everyday language.

We recite. We listen. But following the words and understanding their meaning are different experiences.

What if the meaning could stay in view, as we listen?

Meet Ayah Lens.

Built for Meta glasses, powered by Muse AI, it follows Quran recitation and displays the Arabic alongside its English meaning.

Hands free. As the recitation continues.

## Production notes

Show a population caption: Pew Research Center, 2020 estimate, published 2025. The estimate is about two billion.

Prayer-scene disclaimer: Technology demonstration only. This does not establish religious permissibility for use during prayer.

The requested Arabic-only switch is pending implementation and verification. Do not film a simulated control as working software.

This version is a narrative direction. It is not a completed recording or a word-timed final cut.
