# Ayah Lens v1.0 narration, read sheet for Faraz (2026-11-04)

How to record: Voice Memos on the iPhone, a quiet room, phone about 30 cm from your mouth, the same position for every take. One file per paragraph, named s1 to s7. Two seconds of silence before you start and after you finish each one. Read it as if telling one person, not announcing. The target seconds are a guide from the AI read; your own pace wins, within a few seconds. If a take goes wrong, record it again; keep the best one. Drop all files in Downloads and say "voice in Downloads".

The words in CAPITALS at the end of each paragraph are where the picture cuts; land on them with a slight stop.

## s1, about 14 s (first person; pause one second after the first sentence, the picture changes there)
I'm listening to a reciter on television, wearing Meta's display glasses. This is what I see. The verse appears with its meaning, and when he moves on, the lens follows. I'M NOT TAPPING ANYTHING.

## s2, about 16 s
About two billion Muslims, by Pew's 2020 estimate. Many of us hear the Quran daily and cannot follow the Arabic. Looking a verse up pulls you out of the moment. The meaning should come to you. THIS IS AYAH LENS.

## s3, about 20 s
The Meta Ray-Ban Display glasses listen. Muse Voice writes out the Arabic as it is recited. The software finds the verse, and your place inside it. Muse Spark prepares the match to the published English, and the meaning follows the recitation into the lens. AND THAT IS THE EASY PART.

## s4, about 25 s
The hard part is knowing where you are. More than six thousand verses, phrases that repeat across them, and listeners who join halfway through. So watch this one. Same verse number, the whole way. But the words on the lens keep moving, because Ayah Lens follows your place inside the verse. And if you would rather, the meaning STEPS ASIDE.

## s5, about 11 s
If you are memorizing, the English gets in the way. So switch it off. The Arabic keeps following on its own, and when you want the meaning back, IT RETURNS.

## s6, about 21 s
That is the whole product. Here is what is real. The software was written in ten days. Transcription and tracking run live; only Spark's English pairing is prepared in advance. Fifty-nine tests check the matching, the source text and the frame delivery. WATCH ONE MORE VERSE ARRIVE.

## s7, about 9 s
Many listeners hear these words without following them. Now the meaning can follow. Hear the recitation. Follow the meaning.
